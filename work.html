These illustrative concepts use sample scenarios to show what Wecite could build.

Custom business system

A request tracker for a service team

Suppose a business receives service requests through several channels. Staff need to know who is handling each request and what still needs a response.

What the first version could do

  • Record each request with a customer reference, owner, and due date.
  • Show which requests are new, assigned, or closed.
  • Give managers a view of overdue requests and requests awaiting assignment.

Business website

A website for a local service provider

Suppose customers keep asking about service areas, availability, and how to request a quote. A focused website can answer those questions early.

What the first version could include

  • Service descriptions written around what customers need.
  • A service-area page and answers to common questions.
  • A direct path for customers to contact the business.
