Privacy

How the Wecite website handles project briefs and visits.

Project briefs

The brief builder prepares your text in your browser. It does not send or store your entries on a server. When you copy a brief, your device puts the text on its clipboard. When you download it, your browser saves a text file on your device.

Website visits

Wecite does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies to this website. The hosting provider may process technical information needed to serve the website.

Sharing a brief

If you share a brief through email or another service, that service handles the information you send. Leave passwords and confidential client information out of the brief.
